We build engines, test them, and publish what happened.

CU Propulsion Laboratory is a student club at CU Boulder where members design, machine, test, and operate real propulsion hardware

Join the lab

Hands-on, rigorous, and safe

Our mission is to give members rigorous and safe hands-on experience in designing, manufacturing, testing, and operating the hardware and software of various propulsion systems.

Build what you study

Apply thermodynamics, fluid dynamics, and mechatronics to real machining, fabrication, and testing.

Cross majors

Work in small teams that span disciplines and see engineering beyond your own major.

Keep the knowledge

Documentation and test data carry over, so each team starts where the last one ended.

Transparency

All operations are published in as much detail as possible
