Join the lab

We welcome undergraduate and graduate students from all majors and experience levels at CU Boulder. No prior propulsion background is required—just commitment to rigorous, safe engineering.

1. Submit an interest form

Tell us your major, year, technical interests, and experience.

Fill out the interest form

2. Attend an onboarding meeting

Meet the project leads, review current hardware designs, and get assigned to a project team.

3. Complete safety & workshop training

All members complete relevant lab safety orientation and required ITLL / Idea Forge machine shop certifications before handling hardware.

Read our safety rules

4. Start building

Participate in weekly project team and general club meeting, contribute to CAD, simulation, or fabrication, and help publish semester test reports.

Spring 2027 project teams

Impulse Jet Engine

Thermodynamics, combustion chamber fabrication, fuel delivery, instrumentation, and static test stand operations.

Electric Motor

Electromagnetic design, stator winding, rotor machining, power electronics, and dynamometer testing.
